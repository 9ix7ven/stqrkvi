About Our School

Building confident, capable young minds.

Abhinav Public School, CU Block Pitampura, is a CBSE-affiliated Senior Secondary School established in 2003.

Our approach focuses on academic learning alongside sports, technology, creativity, leadership and practical experiences.

Our Vision “Creating Leaders who are Ambitious, Proficient, and Smart”
